Paper mode:
Tap Play on the title screen, or Paper and then Demo photo.
How to try paper mode
  1. Print the A4 template at 100% and draw on it with a fat black marker, plus red and blue.
  2. Photograph it with a phone (landscape, all four corner marks in view) and press Upload photo, or use Webcam.
  3. No printer? Demo photo draws your current level onto the template through a simulated camera. The stress preset adds dim light, shadows, tilt, noise or blur.
  4. On the Camera check screen watch the four lights, then press the big GO button. The vision stages appear below the screen.
